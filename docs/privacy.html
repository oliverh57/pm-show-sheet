Privacy policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

This policy explains how PM Show Sheet ("the app"), a desktop application made by Pixelmappers, handles your information. In short: the app runs on your computer and talks directly to Google. Pixelmappers does not operate any server for the app and does not collect, receive, store or sell your data.

Information the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, the app requests these permissions:

  • Basic profile (openid, email, profile): your name, email address and profile picture, used only to show which account is signed in.
  • Google Drive (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive): used to open, create and save show files, renders, audio and other media in the Drive folders you choose, and to create and update the Google Sheet and Google Slides files the app publishes for a show.

The app only works with files and folders that are part of the shows you open or create. It does not read, scan or index the rest of your Drive.

How information is stored

  • Your Google sign-in token is stored in the macOS Keychain on your computer.
  • Your name, email address and profile picture address are cached in the app's settings folder on your computer so the app can open offline.
  • Show data and media are stored in your Google Drive and, where you choose, in local folders on your computer.

None of this is sent to Pixelmappers or any third party. Data passes only between your computer and Google's APIs, over encrypted connections.

Sharing

Pixelmappers does not share, sell or transfer your information. Google Sheets, Slides and Drive files the app creates are shared only with the people you choose to share them with in Google Drive.

Google API Services User Data Policy

PM Show Sheet's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the app's features described above. It is not used for advertising, is not transferred to others, and is not read by people except with your permission, for security reasons, or where required by law.

Software updates

The app checks GitHub for new versions on launch and every few hours. This request goes to GitHub and contains no personal information, though GitHub can see your IP address as with any web request. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Removing access and deleting data

  • Signing out in the app revokes its Google access and removes the token from your Keychain.
  • You can also remove access at any time from your Google Account's Third-party apps & services page.
  • Deleting the app and its settings folder removes everything it stored on your computer. Files in your Google Drive stay yours, and you can delete them in Drive.

Children

The app is a professional tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.
