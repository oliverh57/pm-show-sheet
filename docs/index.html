PM Show Sheet

A desktop app by Pixelmappers for building show sheets.

PM Show Sheet is a Mac app for planning a show song by song: running order, renders, audio versions, show records, colours and notes. It keeps each show in your own Google Drive and publishes a Google Sheet and Google Slides deck that collaborators can open.

How it uses your Google account

When you sign in with Google, the app asks for your name, email address and profile picture (to show who is signed in) and access to Google Drive (to read and save show files and media, and to create and update the show's Sheet and Slides). Everything happens directly between the app on your computer and Google. Pixelmappers runs no server and never receives your data. Read the privacy policy for details.

Install (Mac)

Paste into Terminal:

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/oliverh57/pm-show-sheet/main/install.sh | bash
